Adds `llm_d_async_async_deadline_required_replicas`, a per-queue gauge of the replicas needed to finish queued items before their deadlines, for use as a KEDA trigger. It is computed each backlog poll from the deadline-proximity counts and a new optional per-queue `deadline_scaling` block (`throughput_rps`, `cold_start_seconds`, `max_replicas`, `safety_factor`) on the `redis-sortedset` and `sql` transports. Expired items don't count, and a failed deadline read drops the queue's series.
