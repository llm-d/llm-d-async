The Redis transports and the Redis sorted-set producer can connect to a Redis Cluster or a Sentinel-managed deployment. Set `mode` (`standalone`, `cluster` or `sentinel`) with optional `addrs` and `master_name` in the transport config, or the matching `RedisMode`, `RedisAddrs` and `RedisMasterName` producer fields; the URL keeps carrying credentials, TLS and tuning. Standalone deployments keep their existing key names. In cluster mode the private claim-bookkeeping keys are hash-tagged onto their queue or result route, and publishing a result into a list in another hash slot takes two fenced steps instead of one atomic script, so consumers may occasionally see a result twice after a crash (durable result delivery de-duplicates it).
