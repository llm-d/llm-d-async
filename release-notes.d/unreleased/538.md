Adds `llm_d_async_async_pool_deadline_required_replicas`, the replicas a worker pool's deployment needs to finish the queued items of all its queues before their deadlines, for use as a KEDA trigger when queues share a deployment. It is computed each backlog poll from a new optional `deadline_scaling` block on worker pools (`throughput_rps`, `cold_start_seconds`, `max_replicas`, `safety_factor`); a queue's own `deadline_scaling.throughput_rps` overrides the pool's for that queue.
