`llm_d_async_async_inference_latency_time_millis` carries the request's trace as an exemplar (`trace_id`, plus `span_id` of the `process-request` span) when the trace is sampled, and the metrics endpoint serves OpenMetrics to scrapers that ask for it, so a point on a latency panel links to its trace. Scrapers that don't ask get the classic format. On Prometheus 2.x, OpenMetrics scrapes store whole-number bucket bounds as `le="10.0"` rather than `le="10"`.
