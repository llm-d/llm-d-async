Results are binary-safe. A response body that is not valid UTF-8 (audio, images) is base64-encoded in `payload` with `payload_encoding: base64`, and Go consumers can call `ResultMessage.Body()`. Results also carry `content_type` and the response headers matched by `--result-header-prefixes` (chart: `ap.resultHeaderPrefixes`). `--max-response-bytes` (chart: `ap.maxResponseBytes`) fails an oversized response with `INFERENCE_ERROR` without retrying.
