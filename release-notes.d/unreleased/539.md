Prometheus gates (`prometheus-saturation`, `prometheus-budget`, `prometheus-query`) can authenticate to Prometheus. `--prometheus-bearer-token-file` sends a bearer token, re-read on every request, and `--prometheus-ca-cert` trusts a CA bundle (chart: `ap.prometheusAuth.bearerTokenFile`, `ap.prometheusAuth.caCertFile`). This lets gates query OpenShift's Thanos Querier with the pod's service account token and `service-ca.crt`.
