`deploy/install.sh` installs Valkey from the Valkey project's Helm chart at a pinned version instead of the Bitnami charts, and no longer points the processor at a Service that does not exist. `REDIS_CHART` is removed: to use another server, set `DEPLOY_REDIS=false` and provide the `redis-creds` Secret.
