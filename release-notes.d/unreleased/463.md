redis-sortedset: requests sharing a deadline dispatch in submission order, to within 62.5ms, when they are submitted within 36h24m32s of that deadline; requests submitted inside one 62.5ms step, or more than 36h24m32s before the deadline, may dispatch in any order among themselves. Producers stamp `EnqueuedAtMs` and queue scores carry a sub-second fraction, so scores are no longer whole-second deadlines; anything reading the queue by whole-second score ranges should use an exclusive next-second upper bound.
