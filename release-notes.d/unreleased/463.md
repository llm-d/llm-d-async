redis-sortedset: requests sharing a deadline dispatch in submission order, to within 62.5ms; requests submitted inside one 62.5ms step may still dispatch in any order. Producers stamp `EnqueuedAtMs` and queue scores carry a sub-second fraction, so scores are no longer whole-second deadlines; anything reading the queue by whole-second score ranges should use an exclusive next-second upper bound.
