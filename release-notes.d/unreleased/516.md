`producer-sql` adds `ReceiveResult`, `ReceiveResults`, and `AckResult`: results stay in Postgres until acknowledged, and a delivery not acknowledged within `ResultLeaseTTL` (default five minutes) is redelivered. `GetResults` skips results under a live lease. Migration `0002` adds the claim columns; roll out processors before consumers.
