Async tracing uses `gen_ai.request.id`, `gen_ai.request.model`, and the
`llm_d.async.*` namespace, aligned with llm-d-router. Legacy `request.id`,
`queue.id`, `queue.name`, `retry.count`, and `error.category` attributes remain
emitted alongside their replacements for one release as requested in #413.
Update trace queries and dashboards to the new names before legacy attributes
are removed.
