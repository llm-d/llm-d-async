Request transforms can implement the optional `transform.ContextTransform` interface to receive a context (ending at the dispatch attempt's deadline, and cancelled on shutdown) and the dispatch URL. A transform error whose chain carries a `RATE_LIMIT` or `SERVER_ERROR` category is now retried with backoff instead of failing the request. A transform cut off by the request's own deadline reports `DEADLINE_EXCEEDED`; one cut off only by `--request-timeout` is retried, and one interrupted by shutdown is re-enqueued.

`transform.Chain.Apply` is deprecated in favor of `ApplyContext`. It is kept on purpose because it shipped in v1.0.0, and will be removed in a later release.
